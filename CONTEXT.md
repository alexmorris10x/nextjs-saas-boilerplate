# SaaS starter

The starter's shared language for access, feedback and prelaunch signups. Each app should add its own product vocabulary here.

## Language

**User**:
A person with a signed-in app account.
_Avoid_: Customer when no payment has occurred

**Internal account**:
An owner or test account excluded from customer, payer and revenue measurements.
_Avoid_: Paying customer

**Full access**:
Access to the app's paid-tier capabilities, whether paid, internal, lifetime or temporarily complimentary.
_Avoid_: Purchase as a synonym for access

**Feedback pass**:
A limited-use invitation granting time-limited full access for feedback, with no card or payment.
_Avoid_: Coupon, trial subscription

**Redemption**:
A user's one-time acceptance of a particular feedback pass.
_Avoid_: Purchase

**First touch**:
The first recorded visit's referral and campaign details, including a visit with no campaign.
_Avoid_: Latest campaign

**Waitlist signup**:
An email address registered for a prelaunch product and its transactional confirmation.
_Avoid_: User, customer
